# The physics of Credence

One credit body. Every instrument has a physical meaning. Every financial
measurement comes from retained evidence or a chain-derived record. A visual
may explain a law; it must not manufacture the activity the law describes.

Before adding an element, ask: **what is this in the physics?** Then ask:
**which evidence gives it this state?** Missing measurements are unknown,
never zero, healthy, approved, or live by default.

## The object

| Object | Law | Current source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shell | The surface holds agents. Fine stippling and contour lines are material, not an invented population. | Explicitly selectable borrower or comparison profiles only |
| Node | Size follows the square root of capacity so area represents credit limit. Cold light is ordinary; gold is reserved for measured scores ≥ 800. Restricted profiles are hollow. Selected nodes receive an outer ring. | `outcome.capacity.agent_capacity`, `score`, and `decision` |
| Core | Available lender cash is the light inside. Its brightness follows idle cash divided by idle cash plus outstanding principal. It is brightest when cash is available and dims as it leaves. | Vault idle cash and outstanding-principal checks |
| Atmosphere | Thickness is `1 - utilization`. Unknown book data draws no reassuring halo. | Outstanding principal divided by idle cash plus outstanding principal |
| Arc | An origination joins lender and borrower. Height follows tenor; width follows principal. Retained paths are historical, not live activity. A user-triggered replay lasts two seconds. | Approved origination assessments; indexed records stay in a separate log |
| Ice caps | Escrow is pale, still and held. Never infer released collateral from a completed animation. | Requires an explicit escrow balance and release record |
| Storm | A storm is a book whose measured utilization exceeds its stated threshold. Do not imply that one static reading describes today's weather. | Per-book utilization; no invented markets or historical weather |
| Scar | A default belongs only to the book or agent that defaulted. Preserve it for at least 90 days; without a timestamp, never infer that it healed. | Realized-loss checks and timestamped default evidence |
| Moon | A moon is an actual retained facility, never an invented market. More moons require more independently identified books. Distance can encode risk only with comparable risk evidence. | This release has one retained facility |
| Satellite | Labels and instruments sit outside the object. Leader lines connect them to what they describe. | The same values as the corresponding record |
| Section | Identity, reputation, escrow and settlement are the four layers. Spacing explains structure; it does not imply an unmeasured quantity. | Architectural diagram; amounts appear only when retained |
| Eclipse | The overlapping shadow grows with measured utilization. Its distance is a projection of that ratio, not a second risk score. | The same utilization as the atmosphere |

The borrower in the repayment run and the comparison profiles are different
sources. A scenario is not another registered production agent. The indexed
log is also independent of the gateway's retained run: never join them by
loan ordinal, or use a result from one to mark the other's loan repaid.

## Rooms

- **Observatory**: full body, retained borrower, historical arcs and instruments.
- **Atlas**: the same projection unrolled, with searchable comparison profiles
  and approved/denied filters. Coordinates are layout, not physical geography.
- **Moons**: the retained facility; selecting it opens a 280px spec sheet while
  the surrounding system stays visible.
- **Body**: the retained development agent, its native score and recorded
  capacity. Do not describe it as the viewer's agent without wallet identity.
- **Arc**: the existing origination and forecast record. A visual replay does
  not originate, sign, repay or otherwise submit a transaction.
- **Log**: indexed journeys first, with a route to the full chain-derived log.
- **Field guide**: Survey, Shell, Core, Weather, Moons and Scars.

All existing assessment, comparison, lender, enforcement, market, evidence,
indexer and deterministic replay records remain available from the field
navigation. Detailed capacity diagrams retain their explicit loan labels;
their history markers must not be mistaken for an agent population.

## Colour and type

Keep the established colours: warm paper `#f9f5ef`, graphite `#1b1d1e`,
teal `#00706f`, moss `#4a6b3d`, and vermilion `#bf3a14`. Preserve the existing
night-theme tokens as well. Teal marks identity and measurement, moss marks
capacity, and vermilion marks refusal and loss. Gold `#c4a574` is used only
for a measured score of at least 800.

Three type families: Space Grotesk for the world and navigation; Instrument
Serif for the intimate agent, dock and guide; IBM Plex Mono for instruments.
Never set the whole product in a monospace font. Instruments use tabular
figures and 9–12px labels; primary copy remains comfortably readable.

The requested 0–850 barometer cannot silently replace the actual risk
engine's 300–900 scale. Show the native scale explicitly and map its full
range to the gauge. Any future score-scale change belongs in the protocol,
not in a stylesheet.

## Motion and interaction

- Constant globe rotation: 18 seconds per revolution. Pause while inspecting
  the object; provide an explicit pause control.
- Projection and camera transitions: approximately 800ms with
  `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` for CSS camera movement.
- Atmosphere and core values ease toward new measurements over two seconds.
- Arc replay: two seconds, explicitly requested by the reader.
- No bounce or spring. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` in CSS and rendering.
- Stop drawing an offscreen or hidden document. Cap canvas pixel density at 2.
- Hold Alt for whisper mode. Release or blur always restores instruments.
- A dock can be dismissed with its close button or Escape. Return focus to
  the instrument that opened it.
- The scrubber walks retained originations, not an invented 30-day history.
  The book remains labelled as the latest survey throughout.

## States and provenance

Loading says the survey is being read. Missing data says which survey source
is unavailable; unrelated sources can still be inspected. A measured empty
book and an unavailable book are different states. A missing route says
“This coordinate is empty” and offers a route back to the observatory.

The deployment label states its chain and environment. A retained snapshot
never becomes a “live” feed through a pulsing light. Capability statuses in
the claim ledger describe implemented capabilities, not live market data.

Do not add simulated signing, wallet registration, independent liquidity,
LLTV, sound, storm histories, 30-day time travel, or moon maturity without
corresponding state and evidence. They are future rooms, not fake controls.

## Copy

Short, physical, precise. “The credit body.” “Every agent is a coordinate.”
“Read the book as a map.” “The core awaits a measurement.” A sentence should
still sound like a place with the product name removed.
